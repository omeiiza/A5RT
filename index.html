Process: how to take a trade

Prime Flow execution timing is flexible. ARC executes in the London session only. No news trading on any model.

Follow the steps in order. Each step must pass before the next one starts.

  1. Read H4 context. Identify H4 order flow (ODFLW), the H4 candle profile, and any H4 liquidity sweep. This is the HTF Bias Layer (Prime Flow).
  2. Choose the model.
    • Prime Flow A+ (H4 reversal): H4 order flow, H4 ML (FVG/IMB, U.Wick, Twin Bar), H4 candle profile, and an H4 liquidity sweep. All four must be present.
    • Prime Flow B+ (H4 engulfing continuation): H4 order flow and H4 candle profile. No sweep needed.
    • ARC: EURUSD or GBPUSD only. Mark the M15 Asia range from 18:00 to 00:45 New York Time, then trade it in the London session only.
  3. Set direction from the model. If no model qualifies, the grade is NO SETUP / X and you do not trade.
  4. Define location. This is the ARC, the ML, or the H4 structure the model points to.
    • ARC with no H4 sweep at execution: RAL. The range is the liquidity zone.
    • ARC with an H4 sweep already done: RAM. The range is the mitigation zone.
  5. Wait for the trigger: M3 SIM + body break. Price touching the ARC, ML, or H4 structure is not an entry.
  6. Execute and label the entry type: SIM, BE, IMB, U.WICK, or T.BAR.
  7. Place the stop-loss at the invalidation high or low. This applies to every entry type.
  8. Set the take-profit at 10R. 10R is also the minimum R:R. If the invalidation stop does not allow 10R, there is no trade.
  9. Size the trade at 0.5% risk from the stop distance. Check you are within 2 trades for the day and above the -2% daily stop.
  10. Manage the trade. Choose one of two methods: trail the stop, or set and forget. Do not trade through news.
  11. Journal it. Log the trade in the current Circle, record the outcome in R-multiples, and assign a grade: A+/CLEAN, A+/EX-ERROR, NO SETUP/X, or MISSED.
